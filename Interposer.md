## Motivation and Utility

The miniDSP MCH Streamer Lite is a useful tool for audio collection. Unfortunately, it's headers have a pitch of 2mm. The Interposer breaks out the MCH Streamer Lite's headers into 2.54mm headers, with one header per channel of audio. The channel-select pin of a microphone plugged in to the Interposer can be set by configuring 0-Ohm resistors on the underside of the Interposer.
